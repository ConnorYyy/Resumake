Frequently asked questions

Is Resumake free to use?

Yes. Creating and exporting resumes is free. Supporting the project with Buy Me a Coffee is optional and unlocks nothing.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the editor and start right away. Your resume is saved in your browser.

Is my resume data uploaded to a server?

No. Resume content, photos and settings stay in your browser. You can save a backup file at any time and restore it on another device.

Can I export my resume as a PDF with selectable text?

Yes. PDF export uses your browser's print function, so the text stays selectable and links remain clickable.

Do you collect any data?

Two things, and neither identifies you. Anonymous crash reports — the error type and a stack trace — help us fix bugs, and never include your resume content, photos or personal details. We also count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, does not track you across other sites and collects no personal data.
