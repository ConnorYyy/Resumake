What is Resumake?

Resumake is a free, browser-based resume builder.

You choose one of three templates, fill in your details, and export an ATS-friendly PDF. There is no account, no installation and no server-side copy of your CV.

It runs entirely in your browser and is available in English and Simplified Chinese. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with resumake.io.

Why choose Resumake over other resume builders?

Most free resume builders ask you to register first, then charge you to download. Resumake does neither.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up wall, no email and no trial period — open the editor and start typing.

Is the PDF really free, with no watermark?

Yes. $0, unlimited exports, and no watermark or logo added to your resume.

Will my PDF pass ATS parsing?

Yes. The PDF is made of real text rather than an image, so applicant tracking systems can read it.

Is my resume uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so there is no resume database to leak.

What's included in the editor?

Simple controls for content, and full control over the layout when you need it.

Can I edit with a form, or with code?

Both. Fill in each section step by step, or switch to Advanced and edit the HTML directly.

Is there a live preview?

Yes. Every change appears instantly on an A4 or Letter page, with page breaks marked as you type.

Can I export selectable text?

Yes. The PDF contains real text and clickable links, with print margins from narrow to wide.

Can I add a photo?

Yes. Upload a photo cropped to a 3:4 ID-photo ratio, align it left, center or right, and choose an accent color.

Is my data private?

Yes by design. Auto-save keeps your draft in this browser, and a backup file lets you restore it anywhere.

Does it support Chinese?

Yes. The interface and resume labels switch between English and Simplified Chinese.

Who is it for?

People who want a clean resume without an account.

It suits students and first-time job seekers, career changers, bilingual applicants writing in English or Chinese, and anyone who would rather not upload a CV to a third-party database.

It is also useful when you are applying from a shared or public computer: you can build, export and leave without signing in to anything.

When should you use it?

Three common use cases.

Applying to one role and needing a clean one-page PDF in a few minutes; tailoring a separate version for each application; and building a resume on a shared or public computer without signing in to anything.

It is also useful when you simply do not want your CV sitting in another company's database.

Frequently asked questions

Is Resumake free to use?

Yes. Every template and unlimited PDF exports cost $0. There is no trial that expires and no paywall at download. Supporting the project with Buy Me a Coffee is optional and unlocks nothing.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no account, no email and no password. Open the editor and start typing; your draft is saved automatically in your browser.

Is my resume data uploaded to a server?

No. Resume content, photos and settings stay in your browser storage. There is no resume database on the server, so there is nothing to leak or sell.

Can I export my resume as a PDF with selectable text?

Yes. Export opens your browser's print dialog, so the PDF is made of real text rather than an image. Text stays selectable, links stay clickable and applicant tracking systems can parse it.

Do you collect any data?

Two things, and neither identifies you. (1) Anonymous crash reports — the error type and a stack trace — used to fix bugs; they never include your resume content, photos or personal details. (2) Anonymous counters: page views via Cloudflare Web Analytics, plus how often the editor is opened or a PDF is exported. No cookies, no identifiers, and nothing about your resume leaves your browser.

Does Resumake add a watermark to the PDF?

No. The exported PDF contains only your resume. There is no watermark, no logo and no footer added by Resumake, and no download counter.

Do I need an email address to use it?

No. Resumake never asks for an email address, does not send verification links and has no password to reset. Nothing is sent to your inbox.

Can I switch templates after I start?

Yes. Switch between Classic, Modern and Minimal at any time. Your content stays the same and the layout is re-rendered instantly.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

It works in modern desktop and tablet browsers. On a phone the editor opens, but the split form-and-preview layout is cramped; a desktop or tablet is the better experience.

Can I export to Word or DOCX?

No. Resumake exports PDF only, because PDF preserves the exact layout recruiters and applicant tracking systems expect.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your draft is stored in this browser, so clearing site data removes it. Use Save backup in the editor to download a JSON file you can restore later or on another device.
