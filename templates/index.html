Three resume templates: Classic, Modern and Minimal.

Each template is single-column, ATS-readable and free. Pick the tone that matches your industry — you can switch at any time without retyping anything.

Try a template Compare them

What does each template look like?

Classic

A centered header, blue dividers and a traditional section order. It reads as safe and familiar, which is exactly what most recruiters in established industries expect.

Modern

A colored header with an accent line under the name. Slightly more visual, still single-column, and suited to technology, product and design-adjacent roles.

Minimal

Generous whitespace and a quiet hierarchy. Nothing competes with the text, so the reader's attention goes straight to your most recent role.

Common questions about the templates.

Which template should I choose?

It depends on 1 thing — the industry. Classic for finance, law and the public sector, Modern for technology and product roles, Minimal for academia and research.

Can I switch templates after I start?

It is instant: switch between the 3 templates at any time, your content is unchanged and the layout re-renders immediately.

Do the templates support Chinese?

It supports 2 languages: all three templates work with English and Simplified Chinese, and section labels can be edited per language.

Are the templates ATS-friendly?

It is: all 3 templates use a single-column structure with standard headings and export a PDF with a real text layer.

Can I change margins and page size?

It allows both: A4 or Letter, 3 margin presets from 10 mm to 20 mm, and a font scale from 80% to 120% so the resume fits on one page.
