Privacy policy

Short version: your resume stays in your browser. This page lists every piece of data the site does touch, in plain language — including the parts that leave your device.

Your resume

Resume content, photos and settings are stored in your browser only — in IndexedDB, under this site's origin. There is no server-side resume database, no account and no upload endpoint, so there is no copy of your CV outside your device.

Clearing site data removes the draft. To keep it, use Save backup in the editor, which downloads a JSON file you control; Import restores it on another device.

The optional AI rewrite button

If you press the AI button, the text of the field you are editing is sent to an AI model through a Cloudflare Worker, together with the interface language and the field name. The request is capped at 600 characters. Your photo, contact details, other sections and the rest of the document are never included.

The response is returned to your browser and not stored. Requests are rate limited per IP address per minute (the counter is discarded), and the API key lives only in the server-side Worker secret. If you never press the button, nothing is sent.

Analytics

Page views are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not fingerprint visitors. In addition, the site records two anonymous counters — the editor was opened, a PDF was exported — by calling its own Worker; only the event name is recorded, never resume content.

There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no cross-site tracking and no user identifier. That is also why you do not see a cookie banner: there are no tracking cookies to consent to.

Crash reports

The editor page loads Sentry to report JavaScript errors. A report contains the error type, a stack trace, the page URL with query strings removed, and browser/version information. It never contains resume content, photos or form field values.

Crash reports are used only to find and fix bugs. The landing pages do not load this library at all.

Third parties

Cloudflare hosts the site and provides the cookieless page-view analytics. Sentry receives the crash reports described above. When you press the AI button, the selected text is processed by the AI provider behind the Worker.

No other third party receives data from this site. There are no ad networks, no data brokers and no marketing tools.

What the site never does

It does not ask for an account, an email address or a password. It does not sell or share personal data. It does not build a profile of you across visits or sites, and it does not put your resume into a training dataset — the AI request is a single field you chose to send.

Your controls

Clear this site's data in your browser to delete the local draft. Skip the AI button to keep everything on your device. Block the analytics script with any content blocker — the site keeps working, because nothing depends on it.

Changes and contact

Frequently asked questions

Does Resumake use cookies?

No tracking cookies. The draft is stored in IndexedDB, not in a cookie, and the analytics service is cookieless. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Is my resume sent to an AI model automatically?

No. Text is sent only when you press the AI button, and only the field you are editing, capped at 600 characters. Nothing else from the resume is included.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. There is no advertising network, no data broker and no analytics vendor beyond the cookieless page-view counter described above.

How do I delete everything?

Clear site data for resumake.online in your browser. That removes the IndexedDB draft and any local settings; there is no server-side copy to delete.
