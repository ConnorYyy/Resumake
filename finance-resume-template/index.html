Finance resume template: numbers first, format second.

Finance hiring is unusually format-sensitive. The content has to show deal exposure, controls and the exact toolkit you have used — and the page has to look conservative. Here is what actually gets read, and which of the three templates to start from.

What a finance resume has to prove

There are 5 signals a finance recruiter checks, in this order: size, instrument, regulatory vocabulary, certification status and length.

  • **Size.** A number attached to what you touched: deal value, portfolio, budget, headcount of a team you supported, volume of transactions.
  • **Instrument.** The thing you built: three-statement model, DCF, LBO, comparable companies, variance analysis, reconciliation, control testing.
  • **Regulatory vocabulary you have actually used.** GAAP or IFRS, SOX, KYC/AML, Basel, MiFID — only if you worked inside it.
  • **Certification status, stated precisely.** “CFA Level II candidate” and “CPA in progress” are honest; “CFA” alone implies a charter you may not hold.
  • **Length.** One page for analysts and associates. Two pages only for 10+ years or managing-director-track roles.

How to write each section

It is 4 sections with 1 rule each: the summary is 2 lines, experience is 3–4 bullets, skills is 3 lines, and education moves with seniority.

**Summary (2 lines).** Role, asset class or function, and one number. “Equity research analyst covering European industrials; published 40+ models across a €12bn coverage universe” beats any adjective.

**Experience (3–4 bullets per role).** Each bullet is verb + instrument + number + outcome. Avoid “responsible for”; nobody hires a responsibility.

**Skills.** Split into three lines — Modelling & analysis, Systems, Languages — instead of one comma soup. A recruiter scanning for “Python” should not have to read a paragraph.

**Education and certifications.** For experienced hires they go after experience; for campus applications they go first, because that is what is being assessed.

Bullet examples that pass the scan

Built the three-statement model and downside case for a **$450M acquisition**, used in the investment committee memo.

Automated the monthly close reconciliation in Python, cutting the cycle from **9 days to 5**.

Ran **120+ KYC reviews** across four jurisdictions with zero findings in the internal audit.

Which template to use

It is 3 templates and 1 rule: Classic for analysts and associates, Minimal for VP and above, and Modern almost never in finance.

  • Classic template — centred header, navy rules — the default for analysts and associates, and the safest for banks, Big Four and public-sector finance.
  • Minimal template — quieter and denser, and the better choice for VP and above, where the page is mostly track record.

Both are single-column with real text, so ATS parsing is identical. The Modern template is rarely the right call in finance — the colour block reads as marketing.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a finance resume be?

One page for analysts and associates, two pages once you are past roughly 10 years or applying at managing-director level. Recruiters in finance generally stop reading after the first page.

Should I list CFA or CPA progress?

Yes, but state it exactly: “CFA Level II candidate” or “CPA in progress (2 of 4 sections passed)”. Writing “CFA” when you do not hold the charter is treated as a misrepresentation in finance.

Do finance resumes need a photo?

In the US and UK, no. In parts of continental Europe and Asia it is still common. The template supports an optional photo if your market expects one.

Is this finance resume template ATS-friendly?

Yes. All three templates are single-column with standard section headings and export to a PDF with a real text layer, which is what applicant tracking systems read.

What if I have no deal experience yet?

Show the closest proxy: modelling courses with a completed model attached, university investment fund work, or audit engagements where you reviewed the numbers. Put the number on whatever you did have.
