A resume builder without email. No verification link, no inbox.

There is no email field, no confirmation step and no mailing list. Your work is saved in the browser you are using, and a backup file covers everything a login normally would.

Start without an email How saving works

How does it work without an email address?

Does Resumake need my email address?

No. The editor never asks for an email address and there is no field for one. You can build and export without entering any contact detail about yourself.

How do I save my resume without an email account?

Your draft is saved automatically in this browser's local storage. To keep a copy elsewhere, use Save backup to download a JSON file.

How do I recover my resume if I lose the device?

Only through the backup file. There is no email-based recovery, because no email address is ever collected.

Will I receive marketing emails?

No. There is no mailing list, because there is no email address to add to one. Nothing is sent to your inbox.

Is there a password or login I could forget?

No. There is no password, no login and no verification step, so there is nothing to forget or reset.

Saving, in plain terms.

Auto-save happens as you type.

The editor writes your content to IndexedDB in this browser. Close the tab, reopen it, and the draft is still there.

The backup file replaces the account.

Save backup downloads a JSON file with your resume and settings. Import it on another computer and the resume reappears exactly as it was.

Treat it like a document: it contains your personal details, so keep it out of public folders and repositories.

What you are trading away.

If your device dies and you have no backup, the draft is gone. That is the price of not having an account — and it is also why there is no database to breach.
