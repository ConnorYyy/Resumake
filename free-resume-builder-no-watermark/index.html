Is it really free?

Is the whole product really free?

It costs $0: every template, unlimited resumes and unlimited PDF exports, with no payment step, no card and no tier to upgrade from.

Does the PDF have a watermark?

It does not: 0 watermarks, 0 logos and no footer added by Resumake — the PDF contains only your resume.

Is there a download limit?

It is unlimited: no counter, no daily cap. Export as many PDFs as you need, including 1 per application.

How can it stay free?

It costs almost nothing to run: 1 static site, no user database and no per-user storage, so there is no sales team, ad network or paywall to fund.

What's actually limited?

Being honest about scope is more useful than a list of vague promises.

What is not included.

There is no AI writing assistant, no DOCX export, no cloud sync and no job-board integrations. The editor has three templates rather than fifty.

If you need AI rewriting or a large template library, a bigger product will serve you better — and most of them will charge for the PDF.

What you get instead.

A fast, private editor that produces a clean one-page PDF with selectable text, in English or Chinese, without an account. That is the whole product, and it is not a demo version.

See the three templates for what the output looks like, or read why the PDF is ATS-friendly.

Where the donation fits in.

The Buy Me a Coffee link in the footer is voluntary and unlocks nothing. It does not remove a watermark, because there is no watermark, and it does not raise a limit, because there is no limit.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Resumake costs $0. Every template, unlimited resumes and unlimited PDF exports are included, with no trial period and no payment step.

Does the PDF have a watermark?

No. The PDF contains only your resume. There is no watermark, no logo and no footer added by Resumake.

Is there a download limit?

No. There is no counter and no daily cap. Export as many PDFs as you need, including multiple versions for different jobs.

What is the catch?

There is no catch. The optional Buy Me a Coffee link unlocks nothing. The honest limitation is scope: Resumake has no AI writing, no DOCX export and no cloud sync.

How can it stay free?

Resumake is a static site with no user database, so hosting costs are very low. There is no sales team, no ad network and no per-user storage to pay for.

Do I need to register to export?

No. There is no account and no email. The export button works on your first visit.
