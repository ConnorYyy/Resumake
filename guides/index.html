Frequently asked questions

Where should I start?

If you are applying through an online portal, read the ATS guide first — a PDF that fails parsing never reaches a human. If you are writing your first resume, start with the one-page guide, then pick a template.

Do these guides only apply to Resumake?

No. The ATS, one-page and role guides describe how resume screening works in general and apply to any builder. Only the pages under "Using Resumake" describe this specific tool.

Is any of this behind a paywall or an account?

No. Every guide is public, and the editor needs no account, no email address and no payment. There is no paid tier to upgrade to.

How do I check that my exported PDF is readable?

Open the PDF, select a line of text and paste it into a text editor. If it pastes as readable text in the right order, an ATS can extract it too. If the whole page selects as one image, it cannot.
