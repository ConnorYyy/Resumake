Three templates, live A4 and Letter preview, ATS-friendly PDF export. Your data stays in this browser.

What you can do here

Choose one of 3 templates, fill in your details, adjust margins between 10 mm and 20 mm and the font scale between 80% and 120%, then export a PDF with selectable text. If the editor does not appear, enable JavaScript in your browser.

How it works

  • Pick a template — Classic, Modern or Minimal. You can switch at any time without losing content.
  • Type the content. Auto-save writes to this browser as you go, and undo/redo covers the last edits.
  • Export with your browser's print dialog: real text, clickable links, A4 or Letter, no watermark.

What this editor does

A free resume editor that runs entirely in the browser. Pick one of three templates, fill in your details, and export an A4 or Letter PDF with a real text layer — the kind applicant tracking systems can actually read. There is no account, no email address and no paywall.

Where your resume is stored

Drafts are saved in this browser only, in IndexedDB, and the server keeps no copy — so there is no resume database to leak. To move a resume to another device, download the JSON backup and import it there. The optional AI improve button is the single exception: it sends at most 600 characters of the field you are editing, and the result is not stored.

Exporting the PDF

Export opens your browser's print dialog. Keep the paper size and margins on default, turn off “Headers and footers”, and choose “Save as PDF”. The file keeps selectable text and clickable links, and nothing is watermarked.
