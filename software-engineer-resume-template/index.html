Software engineer resume template: impact, not a technology list.

Most engineering resumes are a list of frameworks with no evidence that anything shipped. The ones that get interviews answer three questions in the first ten seconds: what did you build, at what scale, and what changed because of it.

What an engineering resume has to prove

There are 5 things a reviewer checks in the first 10 seconds: impact, scope, ownership, stack and length.

  • **Impact in numbers.** Latency, cost, error rate, uptime, conversion — a metric that moved because of your work.
  • **Scope.** How many services, users, requests per second, or people. “Migrated the API” is small until it says 40 services and 12k req/s.
  • **Ownership.** Designed, led, migrated, owned — not “participated in”.
  • **Stack, grouped.** One line per category (languages, infrastructure, data) rather than a keyword dump.
  • **Length.** One page up to about eight years of experience. Two pages after that, and only if both are full.

How to write each section

It is 4 sections; the summary takes 2 lines and the experience bullets do the actual selling.

**Summary (2 lines).** Seniority, domain, and one differentiator. “Backend engineer, 6 years, payments and ledger systems; cut settlement latency 8× at a 40M-transaction-per-day processor.”

**Experience (3–4 bullets per role).** Start with the verb, end with the number. Keep the technology inside the sentence, where it has context, instead of hoisting it into a tag list.

**Projects.** For junior engineers these carry more weight than coursework. Include a repository link — the template supports clickable links in the PDF.

**Skills.** Group by what a hiring manager searches for. If the job description says “TypeScript”, write “TypeScript”, not “TS”.

Bullet examples that pass the scan

Cut p95 API latency from **480 ms to 120 ms** by replacing per-request joins with a materialised view.

Led the migration of **40 services** to a new deployment pipeline, taking release frequency from weekly to **daily**.

Reduced cloud spend **31%** ($1.8M/year) by right-sizing workloads and moving batch jobs to spot capacity.

Frequently asked questions

How many technologies should I list?

Only the ones you would defend in an interview. A grouped skills section of 8–14 items reads as credible; a wall of 40 keywords reads as a course certificate.

Should I include GitHub or a portfolio link?

Yes, if the repositories show real work. The exported PDF keeps links clickable, so a reviewer can open them from the document.

Do side projects belong on a senior resume?

Only when they are relevant or unusual. For senior hires the work experience should carry the page; for juniors and career changers projects often matter more than the internship.

Is this software engineer resume template ATS-friendly?

Yes. Single column, standard section headings, no tables or icons, and a PDF with a real text layer that applicant tracking systems can extract.

One page or two?

One page up to about eight years. Beyond that, two pages are accepted if both are full and the second page is not a list of ancient frameworks.
